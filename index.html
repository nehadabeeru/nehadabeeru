01 · About

Python Backend Software Engineer with 4+ years of experience building production APIs, event-driven services, and distributed data workflows on AWS. Experienced in designing GraphQL and REST APIs, orchestrating asynchronous processing with Lambda, Step Functions, EventBridge, and SQS, and developing reliable persistence layers with PostgreSQL and DynamoDB. Proven track record of resolving production failures, implementing idempotent and retry-safe workflows, and improving system reliability through automated testing, observability, and failure recovery.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of distributed systems and applied GenAI — at Merck, that means clinical-data pipelines that auto-code encoding terms against MedDRA/WHODrug at 99%+ accuracy, and a Bedrock-backed field-mapping engine that cut study onboarding from months to a week. Earlier, at Bank of America, I built the backend and data-migration tooling behind large-scale financial-risk and Cards & Payments systems — REST APIs, reconciliation engines, and event-triggered pipelines that replaced always-on infrastructure.

I care about the parts of engineering that don't show up in a demo: idempotency, retry safety, structured logging, and the kind of test coverage that catches a regression before a customer does.
