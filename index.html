DataFlo & DQ Checker

production

GenAI clinical data platform

Problem

Mapping clinical study fields to CDASH standards was a manual, 8–10 week process per study, gating downstream regulatory submission work.

Architecture

Input schema→ pgvector candidate retrieval→ Claude/OpenAI reranking→ Confidence score→ Auto-map / human review→ Persisted mapping + audit trail

Multi-model reranking combines model agreement with confidence thresholds; low-confidence or conflicting results fall back to a secondary model or human review instead of being automatically applied.

GenAI design

pgvector similarity search retrieves candidate CDASH matches; AWS Bedrock (Claude Haiku) reranks to confirm the best match using prompt-engineered comparison logic.

Backend & safety

PostgreSQL/DynamoDB-backed persistence with a confidence-tiered mapping workflow — high-confidence matches auto-apply, low-confidence matches route to human-in-the-loop review, balancing speed with accuracy for regulatory readiness. DQ Checker adds a GenAI rule engine so SMEs author 100+ data quality checks in plain language instead of manual rule-scripting.

Impact

Reduced study onboarding from 8–10 weeks to 1 week.

MedPredict

shipped

Hospital readmission prediction system with LLMs

Dataset & model pipeline

100GB of MIMIC-III clinical data (40K+ records), processed through PubMedBERT, ClinicalXLNet, and BigBird transformer models for embeddings and classification.

Clinical record→ Transformer embedding→ FAISS retrieval→ PyTorch classifier→ FastAPI inference API→ AWS deployment

Retrieval & serving

FAISS retrieval feeds a PyTorch classifier for readmission risk; a separate GPT-4-backed RAG prototype surfaces actionable clinical insights on top of the prediction, improving decision-making efficiency by ~80%.

Deployment

Full-stack, Dockerized — Angular frontend, FastAPI backend, Firebase auth, and AWS S3 for clinical data storage — designed with HIPAA-aligned data-handling practices for sensitive clinical data.

Evaluation & what I built

92%+ readmission-risk accuracy at ~2s inference latency. I personally implemented the FAISS retrieval layer, the FastAPI serving path, the Docker/AWS deployment, and the RAG prototype integration.
