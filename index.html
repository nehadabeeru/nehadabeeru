03 · Case Study

Specification Management System

Merck Sharp & Dohme · Jan 2025 – Present

Problem

Encoding specifications for clinical trials were managed through Excel and manual handoffs between business leads, technical architects, and encoding specialists — slow, error-prone, and impossible to audit.

My role

Requirements gathering, architecture, implementation, UI design, and stakeholder demos — owned the product end-to-end from the first conversation with business leads through production rollout.

Architecture & features

Draft → Publish → Promote workflow with role-based access control, full audit history, versioning, and explicit approval states replacing ad-hoc spreadsheet edits.

Engineering decisions

Avoided redundant file storage by sending raw data instead of Excel exports; separated approval concerns into distinct workflow states; kept scope manageable as requirements shifted with the customer's evolving organizational model.

Impact

Replaced a fully manual, spreadsheet-based workflow with a governed application — bringing versioning, audit trails, and role-based access to a process that previously had none.

04 · Projects

DataFlo & DQ Checker

production

GenAI clinical data platform

Problem

Mapping clinical study fields to CDASH standards was a manual, 8–10 week process per study, gating downstream regulatory submission work.

Architecture

Input schema→ pgvector candidate retrieval→ Claude/OpenAI reranking→ Confidence score→ Auto-map / human review→ Persisted mapping + audit trail

Multi-model reranking with confidence thresholds means a single model's blind spot or an outage doesn't stall the pipeline — low-confidence or disagreeing results fall back to a second model or a human reviewer instead of silently shipping a wrong mapping.

GenAI design

pgvector similarity search retrieves candidate CDASH matches; AWS Bedrock (Claude Haiku) reranks to confirm the best match using prompt-engineered comparison logic.

Backend & safety

PostgreSQL/DynamoDB-backed persistence with a confidence-tiered mapping workflow — high-confidence matches auto-apply, low-confidence matches route to human-in-the-loop review, balancing speed with accuracy for regulatory readiness. DQ Checker adds a GenAI rule engine so SMEs author 100+ data quality checks in plain language instead of manual rule-scripting.

Impact

Reduced study onboarding from 8–10 weeks to 1 week.
